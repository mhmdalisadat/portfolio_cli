description: Self-hosted transactional mail relay with DKIM and rate limiting.

## Problem

Third-party email APIs added cost, latency, and external dependency for core transactional flows (password reset, alerts).

## System Design

Postfix relay with SASL auth for trusted senders. OpenDKIM signs outbound mail. Rspamd handles spam scoring and rate limits. Queue monitoring with deferred-mail alerts.

## Architecture Decisions

- Internal relay only — no public MX, outbound-only
- SPF + DKIM + DMARC DNS records configured from day one
- Dockerized for reproducible deployment

## Tradeoffs

- Deliverability requires ongoing reputation management
- Self-hosted means we own spam/abuse prevention
- No vendor SLA — we are the on-call for mail delivery

## Production Behavior

Queue backlog alerts at 100 deferred messages. DKIM pass rate monitored. Bounce handling via log parsing.

## Lessons Learned

Email infrastructure is never "set and forget." Monitor deliverability like you monitor uptime.
